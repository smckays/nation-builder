Chapter the Fourteenth · Kemrath

The bad year

Year 14 of the Republic

No rain fell from April to September. The wells went first, then the river, then everyone's patience. The harvest will be a quarter of what the Vael family needs. The ox was sold in July to pay the tax on the six acres.

Maret walked three miles to the district office to ask for relief. The clerk said the emergency fund had been spent on roads. He was sorry. He wrote the family's name in a book.

The district's largest landowner has a deep well that still runs. He sells water at a price no one in the valley can pay.

Jorin has heard about men in other districts who stopped asking politely.
